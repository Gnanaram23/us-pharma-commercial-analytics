**Estimated:** In 2024, 1.1 million promotional payments totaling $54.4M reached 154,019 HCPs [F1] [F2] [F3]. Novo Nordisk and Lilly reached 57% and 49%, respectively, of top-decile GLP-1 prescribers [F6] [F7]. Only 28% of bottom-half prescribers received any payment [F8].

**How:** Real data from CMS Open Payments and Medicare Part D claims were joined to measure reach and effect. A simulation with 20,000 prescribers over 52 weeks tested attribution methods and next-best-action policies.

**How far to trust it:** The simulation's known true effects validate methods. Attribution methods misallocate about 30% of credit [F17] [F18] [F19] [F20] [F21]. Email targeting on uplift captures 78% of email's total effect [F24]. The contextual bandit policy outperforms fixed rules by 14% [F27] [F28] [F29] [F30]. Limitations include Open Payments' lower-bound reach and potential targeting biases [F9] [F11] [F12] [F13] [F14] [F15] [F16].
