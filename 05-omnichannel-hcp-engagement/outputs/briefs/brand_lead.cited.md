**Promotion to prescribers is not reaching the top performers and attribution methods are inaccurate.**

What we found:
- 97% of 2024 promotional payments were meals, but speaker programs took 51% of the dollars.
- Novo Nordisk reached 57% and Lilly 49% of top-decile GLP-1 prescribers, while only 28% of the bottom half received any payment.
- Every standard attribution method misallocates about 30% of the credit.
- Targeting emails based on uplift scores captures 78% of email's total effect, compared to 67% for likelihood to adopt.

What to do:
- Rebalance promotional reach to the top prescribers before adding more spend.
- Use uplift scores to target emails and cap brand emails at about three per month.

What could change this:
- Reps might be targeting prescribers who are already interested, inflating the impact of promotion.
- The simulation's assumptions about channel effects and fatigue could differ from real-world conditions.
