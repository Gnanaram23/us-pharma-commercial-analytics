**Medicaid coverage removal led to an 88% drop in use in North Carolina, and other states saw similar drops.**

What we found:
- Coverage removal led to an 88% drop in use in North Carolina.
- Other states like California, Pennsylvania, and New Hampshire saw similar drops.
- Restarting coverage in North Carolina only recovered 57% of use.
- There was no evidence of GLP-1 use shifting from obesity to diabetes.

What to do:
- Model the cost of coverage gaps, not just their duration.
- Pre-register a difference-in-differences (DiD) design with pre-period adjustment for pilots.

What could change this:
- Smaller states may be under-counted due to data suppression.
- The newest quarter's data may be revised upwards, affecting rates.
