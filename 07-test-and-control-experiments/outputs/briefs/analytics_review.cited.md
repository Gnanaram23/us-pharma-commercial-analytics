**Estimated:** North Carolina's GLP-1 use for obesity fell 88% [F4] after coverage ended in 2025 Q4, from 165.1 [F1] to 18.7 [F2] prescriptions per 10,000 Medicaid prescriptions. California, Pennsylvania, and New Hampshire saw drops of 75–93% [F5][F6][F7] in their first quarter without coverage. When coverage resumed in North Carolina, use recovered to 57% [F18] of the pre-gap level in 2026 Q1.

**How:** The analysis used synthetic control and difference-in-differences (DiD) methods. Synthetic control was fitted on log rates with the pre-period mean removed, allowing for accurate matching even in states with high GLP-1 use. DiD was adjusted for each state's pre-trend.

**How far to trust it:** Placebo tests showed no spurious drops larger than 34% [F16] or gains larger than 15% [F17], validating the real impact of coverage changes. The 95% confidence interval for the spillover effect on diabetes GLP-1 use ranged from −5.9% [F20] to +2.4% [F21], indicating no evidence of substitution. The synthetic control method tracked the pre-period within 3.3% [F31] log RMSE, confirming its accuracy. However, the analysis relies on suppressed counts for small states, and the newest quarter's data may be revised upwards.
