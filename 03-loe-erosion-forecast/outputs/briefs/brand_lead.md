*What we found:*
- Most small-molecule brands keep about 20% of prescriptions a year after generics enter the market.
- The model underestimated Brilinta's erosion by 19 points on average.
- Biologics like Humira erode much slower, retaining about 72% of prescriptions two years after biosimilars enter.
- Eliquis is expected to remain preferred on Medicaid due to free supply terms, with prescriptions potentially staying at 1.9 million in 2029.

*What to do:*

- Focus commercial and Medicare strategies to mitigate revenue loss from 2028.
