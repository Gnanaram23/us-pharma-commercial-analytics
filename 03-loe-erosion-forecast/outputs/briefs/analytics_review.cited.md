**Loss of Exclusivity: How Fast Brands Erode, and What Happens to Eliquis in 2028**

Some brands retain 64%–78% [F2]. Eliquis, made free to Medicaid in 2026, could retain 1.9 million prescriptions in 2029 [F33].

**How:** 
- Used Medicaid State Drug Utilization Data (2017–Q1 2026) [F1].
- Fit brand share erosion curves with a Bayesian hierarchical model [F50].
- Validated models on Brilinta and Entresto [F4].

**How far to trust it:** 
- Mean absolute error for Entresto was 6 points [F5], for Brilinta 19 points [F6].
- Conformal prediction intervals had average half-widths of 26–32 points [F63] [F60].
- Known weaknesses: Medicaid data may not reflect commercial insurance behavior [project write-up].
