*What we found:*
- Most small-molecule brands keep about 20% of prescriptions a year after generics enter the market [F3].
- The model underestimated Brilinta's erosion by 19 points on average [F6].
- Biologics like Humira erode much slower, retaining about 72% of prescriptions two years after biosimilars enter [F34].
- Eliquis is expected to remain preferred on Medicaid due to free supply terms, with prescriptions potentially staying at 1.9 million in 2029 [F33].
