**About ten new obesity drugs are expected to reach US approval by 2030, mostly arriving in 2029–2030.**

What we found:
- Investment in obesity drugs has surged, with over 130 new trials starting in 2025.
- Lilly and Novo Nordisk account for nearly half of patients in late-stage trials.
- China is now a major source of new obesity drug trials, with 36% of new trials having a site there in 2025.

What to do:
